# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ClaudeAmp welcomes constructive participation from people of every background
and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent, discuss the work rather
than the person, and make room for others to contribute.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, publishing another person's private information, sustained personal
attacks, or deliberately disrupting project spaces.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, release threads,
and other spaces where someone is representing ClaudeAmp. Maintainers may edit
or remove contributions that violate it and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Do not report sensitive conduct matters in a public issue. Contact the
[repository maintainer](https://github.com/petehottelet) privately through the
contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and
confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.
